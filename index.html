Creator Essentials

A short checklist for strong, usable footage.

Keep your own voice and creative style. These essentials help the product look clear, immersive, and naturally connected to your story.

Required projection coverage

Film black-and-white first.

Black-and-white projections are often the clearest and most visually striking footage, so they should be part of every content set.

  • Always capture the black-and-white projection.
  • If your package includes both black-and-white and color discs, film both—color-only footage is not sufficient.
  • For each featured disc, include a full projection, a person in the scene, a natural activity, and a brief product interaction.

Must capture

  • 1080P or higher, stable and in focus
  • A clear before-and-after room change
  • Wide or medium shots with people inside the projection
  • Close-ups of the product and projection details
  • Multiple projection discs when available

Make it feel human

  • Open from a real need, mood, routine, or moment
  • Show natural reactions, movement, and interaction
  • Let benefits appear through the story
  • Slow the pacing after the projection turns on
  • Use a natural CTA connected to the story

Before you post

  • Publish only after final approval
  • Follow the approved direction and essential edits
  • Include your affiliate link and creator discount code
  • Include the required campaign hashtags and official account tags
  • Keep claims grounded in personal experience and environment
  • Avoid medical, sleep, hormone, or nervous-system promises
Please avoid

Unboxing-led videos, product-only footage, logo-only covers, heavy filters, overly promotional language, small or blurry projection areas, and unrealistic product claims.
